My Projects

GIS · ARCMAP

Solar Energy Site Suitability Analysis

Used GIS to assess vacant land parcels in Al-Ahsa using parcel area, slope, solar radiation (GHI), and access to major roads. The analysis identified 51 potential sites.

51 candidate sitesAl-AhsaArcMap

Criteria: vacant plots ≥ 2,000 m²; slope ≤ 5° (DEM); GHI from Global Solar Atlas, summarized by Majority; and major roads ≤ 1 km (Near). Final output: a map of 51 candidate sites. Original map will be added.
